# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in qualiow to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, including on bug reports and false positives.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people using the tool.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information, including confidential session output, without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@willcoliveira](https://github.com/willcoliveira),
through GitHub, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Please do not
report conduct issues in a public issue. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, following the
Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
